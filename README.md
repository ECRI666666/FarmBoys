# 🧑‍🌾 FarmBoys

A charming, vanilla-friendly companion mod for **Minecraft 26.3** running on **NeoForge**.

Say hello to the **FarmBoys**: a trio of adorable, slightly clumsy autonomous robot helpers that take care of your garden and farmland while you go adventuring!

---

## 🤖 Meet the Boys

### 1. 🚿 CanBoy (The Watering Can)
* **Role:** Farmland Hydration & Crop Booster
* **What he does:** Waddles across tilled fields, tilts forward, and sprays dry farmland with water so it stays permanently hydrated (no ugly water holes required in your fields!). Occasionally treats growing crops to a refreshing growth boost with happy particles.
* **Clumsy trait:** Every now and then, he slips on his own fresh water puddle, spins comically in a circle with a squeak, and shakes himself off before getting back to work.

### 2. 🌾 HarvesterBoy (The Crop Reaper)
* **Role:** Crop Harvesting
* **What he does:** Patrols your farm, detects fully ripe wheat, carrots, potatoes, and beetroot, and cleanly snips them down with his spinning blades. Drops the harvest on the ground for ChestBoy.
* **Clumsy trait:** Sometimes he snips with such enthusiastic momentum that he suffers a comical mechanical recoil, hops backward half a block with a puff of smoke, and shakes his head before continuing.

### 3. 📦 ChestBoy (The Seed Planter & Carrier)
* **Role:** Seed Sowing & Harvest Delivery
* **What he does:** 
  1. **Vacuum:** Sucks up all crops and seeds dropped on the ground in a 24-block radius, as well as any items he walks past.
  2. **Planter:** Sows seeds (Wheat, Carrots, Potatoes, Beetroot, Melons, Pumpkins, Torchflower, Pitcher Pods) onto any empty tilled farmland.
  3. **Courier:** Walks over to the nearest chest or barrel, pops open his lid, and neatly deposits the harvest into the container (while keeping a seed reserve so he can keep replanting).
* **Clumsy trait:** Occasionally trips on a pebble while running with a full chest! His lid pops open with a clang, an item falls out, and he stops in panic to quickly pick it back up.

## 🔨 Crafting Recipes

All three bots can be crafted at any standard Crafting Table:

### CanBoy
* **Top:** Copper Ingot | Water Bucket | Copper Ingot
* **Middle:** Copper Ingot | Redstone Dust | Copper Ingot
* **Bottom:** Iron Ingot | Iron Ingot | Iron Ingot

### HarvesterBoy
* **Top:** Iron Ingot | Shears | Iron Ingot
* **Middle:** Iron Ingot | Redstone Dust | Iron Ingot
* **Bottom:** Copper Ingot | Copper Ingot | Copper Ingot

### ChestBoy
* **Top:** Oak Planks | Chest | Oak Planks
* **Middle:** Oak Planks | Redstone Dust | Oak Planks
* **Bottom:** Iron Ingot | Iron Ingot | Iron Ingot

## ⚙️ Configuration & Spawning
* **Natural Spawning Disabled by Default:** Bots only appear when crafted and placed by a player.
* **In-Game Toggle:** Easily enable or disable natural spawning near village farmlands via the NeoForge mod config menu (**Mods ➔ FarmBoys ➔ Config**) or in `config/farmboys-local.toml`.
